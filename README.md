# Amazon-Prime-Video-Content-Analysis

As part of my data analysis portfolio, I conducted an extensive exploration of Amazon Prime Video’s content, focusing on key metrics that provide insights into the platform’s content library. Using tools like Python and data visualization libraries, I analyzed several aspects to uncover trends and patterns that can inform business strategies in the streaming industry. The highlights of the analysis include:

Content Overview: Total number of movies and shows available on Amazon Prime.
Content Ratings: A breakdown of distribution across content ratings like G, PG, and R.
Top-Rated Content: Identification of the top 10 movies and shows with the highest IMDb scores.
Content Growth: Analysis of the number of titles produced by Amazon Prime year by year.
Genre Trends: Most popular genres in terms of production and IMDb scores.
Top Genres and Ratings: A closer look at the genres with the highest IMDb scores and TMDB popularity.
Runtime and Ratings: Exploring the distribution of content runtime and its correlation with IMDb ratings.
Country of Origin: An examination of the top production countries and their average ratings.
Time-Based Trends: Trends in IMDb and TMDB scores, votes, and content popularity over time.
Impact Analysis: Understanding the impact of runtime and content rating on IMDb scores.

This project provided a comprehensive view of the content on Amazon Prime Video, helping to uncover patterns in viewer preferences, genre popularity, and quality across various dimensions.

Through this analysis, I developed skills in data manipulation, exploratory data analysis, and visualization, helping to derive actionable insights from large datasets.
